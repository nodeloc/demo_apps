Hold the line against wave after wave of raiders. Steer with **← →** or **A D**, fire with **space**, pause with **P**, mute with **M**. On a phone, drag to fly — firing is automatic.

Every sound is built by the page out of oscillators and noise, so there is nothing to download and nothing to wait for. It is cosmetic in the strictest sense: the audio reads the same frame events the explosions do and never writes back, so a muted run and a loud one score the same.

Shoot six in a row without being hit and your multiplier climbs, up to ×5. Purple hulks drop **P** for a wider gun and, rarely, a **+** for an extra life. Every fifth wave sends a warlord.

**About the leaderboard.** Your browser never tells the site what you scored. It is handed a seed, it records which keys you held on which frame, and at the end it sends back only those two things. The server replays your run and reads the score off its own copy of the game. The only way onto the board is to actually fly it.
