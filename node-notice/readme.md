# Node notice

Posts your node's standing notice under new topics, and keeps it above the
replies.

The rules, what belongs here and what doesn't, where to ask for help — the
things a node owner ends up typing by hand for the twentieth time. Write it
once here.

## Setting it up

Install it in your node, fill in the notice, and it starts with the next topic.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Notice** | What to say, in your own words. Markdown works. `{username}` becomes the author's name. |
| **Show it to** | `newcomers` says it to each person once, the first time they open a topic here. `everyone` says it on every topic. |
| **Keep it at the top** | Pins the notice so it stays above the replies. |
| **Skip staff topics** | Leaves staff topics alone. They wrote the rules. |

## Which audience to pick

`newcomers` is the default, and it is the right one most of the time. A notice
that appears on every topic gets read once and then stops being seen —
regulars learn to scroll past it, and by the time you actually need it read,
nobody is reading it.

Choose `everyone` when the point is not that people learn the rule but that
nobody can say they weren't told: a node where the notice is a disclaimer, a
warning about scams, a legal line.

Switching from `everyone` back to `newcomers` does not start over. It
remembers who it has already told either way.

## Notes

- Pinning needs the app installed **in a node**, not site-wide — a site-wide
  install has no node whose moderators it acts as. Turn "Keep it at the top"
  off for a site-wide install, or install it per node.
- Every notice ends with a line saying it came from a bot and where to take a
  question. That line is not optional and is written in your node's language.
  A notice nobody can reply to is worse than no notice.
- It only ever posts under the **first** post of a topic, never under replies.
